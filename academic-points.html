GPA = Σ(course credits × grade points) ÷ Σ(registered credits)

Earned credits come from courses cleared with P or higher. An F contributes zero points and zero earned credits, but its registered credits remain in the GPA denominator.

Example: O in a 4-credit course and F in another 4-credit course gives 8 registered credits, 4 earned credits, and GPA 5.00.

SGPA covers one semester; CGPA covers completed semesters. The calculator’s live result includes all entered registered credits and is provisional while grades are missing; missing grades are not recorded as F.

Special pass/fail-only courses, W (withdrawn) and I (incomplete) do not carry GPA points. Do not enter these as ordinary P or F in this calculator. Non-academic participation credits also do not affect GPA/CGPA.

Attendance and downgrading · Section 6
  • 75% or more: no attendance downgrade.
  • 65% to below 75%: normally one grade down in that course: O → A → B → C → D → P → F. The effect on overall GPA depends on that course’s credits.
  • Below 65%: F is the default. Exceptional relaxation for proven reasons is case-specific, forwarded by the Dean (Academics) and approved by the Director.
  • Documented medical cases in the 65–75% range may receive exemption under institute norms. If accepted attendance remains below 75% after medical claims, the page still specifies a one-grade reduction. Do not assume a submitted certificate automatically removes the penalty; follow the institute’s confirmed decision.
  • At least 50% physical in-class attendance is required to be considered for medical or institute-activity leave. Approval cannot bypass this physical-attendance floor.

The calculator estimates standard physical-attendance rules using fixed semester totals. For an approved exception, turn GPA attendance adjustment off and enter the final subject grade confirmed by the institute.

Medical leave, events and withdrawal · Sections 6.2–6.5
  • Sports, cultural events and programming contests count as absences unless prior permission is sanctioned by the Dean or appropriate authority using authenticated documents.
  • Medical absence requires an OHC doctor’s certificate. Certificates issued outside Sri City/the institute must be verified by OHC.
  • For short medical absence, submit certificates through the Academic Office to the Dean (Academics) within two working days of resuming classes.
  • For prolonged illness, also notify the Dean through the Academic Office; submit certificates within two working days of returning.
  • More than 18 working days of valid medical absence can support an application to withdraw from the whole semester. A committee considers it, with recommendation through the Dean to the Director; withdrawal is not automatic.
  • An I grade normally needs conversion within 21 days of the semester’s last working date. An extension to the first week of the next semester requires Dean approval on the instructor’s recommendation.
Exams, re-exams and grade review · Sections 2.5, 4
  • Instructors announce assessment components and weights at the start of a course. Continuous assessment, labs and projects may count alongside exams.
  • Raise mid-exam marking discrepancies when scripts are shown. Make-up quizzes/assignments are at the instructor’s discretion, not automatically scheduled by the Academic Office.
  • F grades and qualifying missed exams have re-exam opportunities in January/July. If still unsuccessful, repeat a core course when offered; for an elective, repeat it or choose another elective.
  • The newer guidelines give two grade-review windows: Section 2.5 mentions one week from the next semester’s start; Section 4.4 mentions two weeks. Contact the instructor within one week and confirm the applicable deadline rather than relying on the longer window.
  • Disciplinary action or insufficient attendance can lead to end-exam debarment. The calculator does not determine exam eligibility.
Promotion, probation and graduation · Sections 4.2, 5
  • Minimum graduation CGPA: 5.0. A CGPA below 5.0 puts a student on probation.
  • All first-semester students may register for semester 2 irrespective of CGPA or F grades.
  • Promotion from UG1 to UG2 requires earning at least 50% of prescribed first-year credits after re-exams. Otherwise, the student may register afresh in year 1, subject to the six-year limit; first year cannot be repeated more than twice.
  • Published totals: B.Tech 144 credits; Honours 152; Specialization 158; Honours with Specialization 166. Required course categories must also be satisfied—total credits alone are insufficient.
  • Normal duration is four years; maximum is six from admission. Medical semester withdrawal still counts toward the maximum.
  • Graduation also requires clearing dues and having no pending disciplinary action. Formal departure before graduation does not provide automatic re-entry; the page specifies the JoSAA/CSAB route.
Registration and semester leave · Section 3
  • Register before the semester within the notified period and clear fees/dues. Missing registration can lead to Academic Office allocation of courses.
  • Normally register for at least 8 credits when that many remain, and no more than the published “24/25” limit. Confirm the applicable cap; exceptions need prior Dean approval.
  • Courses normally require ten registrations, subject to institute discretion.
  • Semester/year leave can be requested after completing year 2 with formal approval; the six-year limit remains. Register in the semester in which you receive the degree.
Projects, Honours, specialization and internships · Sections 3.6–3.11
  • From 2026, the optional B.Tech project may be replaced through approved SWAYAM/NPTEL/Academic Bank of Credits courses. Confirm course equivalence with the Academic Office.
  • Honours runs over four consecutive semesters, may start from year 2, and includes the B.Tech project. Entry requires no first-year backlog; exit requires at least 8.0 in the four-semester Honours work and a qualifying publication, filed patent or demonstrable prototype assessed by a committee.
  • One specialization can be added after year 2: 14 extra credits across three electives, a project and a seminar.
  • Up to two electives may be replaced by faculty-supervised additional projects, at most one per semester.
  • A final-year semester-long project replaces 8 credits for one semester. Inform both Academic and Placement Offices before the semester and obtain the Academic Office NOC.
  • For non-SLP internship cases, the Responsible Student Option offers an attendance-waiver route. Exams remain on schedule and continuous evaluation is arranged with the instructor. Follow the approved RSO process, not the ordinary attendance calculator.
Participation, conduct and help · Section 7

First-year participation in Sports/NSS/UBA or similar activities earns verified non-academic credits, recorded separately from GPA. Follow classroom, lab and examination conduct rules. Ragging and misconduct can lead to disciplinary action; gender-related misconduct is handled through the Internal Complaints Cell.

Start with the course instructor for evaluation/course questions, and the Academic Office for registration, leave and approvals. The institute can amend these guidelines; later applicable notices take precedence.
