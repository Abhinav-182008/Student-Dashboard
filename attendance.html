Attendance rules

75% or above: no penalty. 65–below 75%: one subject grade down, unless the institute confirms medical exemption. Below 65%: F by default; exceptional relaxation is case-specific, forwarded by the Dean and approved by the Director. Medical/activity leave needs at least 50% physical attendance. This tool shows the standard physical-attendance outcome. Official medical/activity exemptions are decided by the institute and are not applied here.
