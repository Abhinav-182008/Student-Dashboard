GPA = Σ(credits × grade points) ÷ registered credits. Registered course credits are the fixed course weight, not credits acquired. Passing earns all of a course’s credits; F earns zero. Only P or higher earns credits. F stays in the GPA denominator. Pending grades are not F; the live score is provisional until all grades are entered. Important academic points →
